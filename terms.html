Terms, Conditions & Policies.

01Rescheduling & Attendance Policy

Students MUST reschedule at least 24-48 hours in advance, adjusted for global timezones.

No-shows without notice are automatically marked as 'used' and deducted from the pack.

Aligns with our trainer 2-day notice rule.

02Pack Expiry & Validity

All sessions in a monthly pack MUST be used within 38 days of purchase to prevent schedule disruption.

This policy prevents schedule disruption and ensures consistent learning momentum.

03Refund Policy

Once a pack is purchased, it is strictly NON-REFUNDABLE. Sessions can only be rescheduled with proper notice.

04Payment & Transactions

We use Razorpay for secure, all-inclusive pricing.

International Bank Transfers are preferred to minimize processing fees.

05Post-Termination (For Trainers/Staff)

Post-termination: All company info/assets must be returned. Former staff cannot represent themselves as connected to Mathify. No personal contact details sharing between trainers and students.

Final invoice processing depends on the return of materials and clearance of obligations.
