Refund Policy.

01Refunds

Once a pack is purchased, it is strictly NON-REFUNDABLE. Sessions can be rescheduled with 24-48 hours notice. No-shows are marked as 'used'.

Rescheduling is allowed within the notice window; refunds are not.

02Rescheduling & Attendance

Students must reschedule at least 24-48 hours in advance.

If a student no-shows without notice, the session is automatically marked as 'used' and deducted from the pack.

03Pack Expiry & Validity

All sessions in a monthly pack must be used within 38 days of purchase.
